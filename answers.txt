Question 1.a -

Agile Pros and Cons:
1.) Pro: Agile is adaptable to changing requirements, 
    as it involves constant collaboration with customers.
2.) Con: It can sometimes be difficult to predict exact timelines
    and budgets without strict management and planning.

Non-Agile Pros and Cons:
1.) Pro: More predictable budget and schedule with clearly defined
    milestones.
2.) Con: Not as flexible to changes as compared with Agile.


Question 1.b -

The Scrum Master is responsible for all scrum related tasks, including 
JIRA tooling and facilitating springs and meetings. The Product Owner 
sets the direction for the team and handles releases and stakeholder 
management.


Question 1.c -

During each sprit there are 3 types of meetings:

1. Sprint Planning Meeting: In this meeting, the team determines what 
needs to be completed in the coming sprint. They also move stories in 
the sprint scope from backlog to current sprint.

2. Daily Scrum/Standup Meeting: This is a short meeting that happens 
every day of the print. Team members talk about work completed and 
plans for the day.

3. Sprint Retrospective Meeting: This occurs at the end of the spring. 
Team members discuss work completed and move incomplete work to backlog. 
They then close the sprint.
